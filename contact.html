Our Story

Your Trusted Local Repair Shop

Phone ElectriK is an electronics repair shop located in Torrance, CA. We service Apple, Samsung, Motorola, LG, OnePlus, TCL, Nokia, and many more makes and models — most repairs are done while you wait.

With over 10 years of hands-on experience, we built Phone ElectriK around three values the repair industry often overlooks: Affordable Prices, Quality, and Honesty. We tell you exactly what's wrong and what it costs — no surprises, no pressure.

We proudly serve the South Bay Area — Torrance, Redondo Beach, Hermosa Beach, Manhattan Beach, El Segundo, Lomita, Wilmington, Long Beach, Gardena, Palos Verdes Peninsula, Carson, Compton, Lawndale, Hawthorne and surrounding communities.

We also carry accessories and refurbished devices in store. Stop by and see what we have — walk-ins are always welcome.
