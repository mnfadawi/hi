iPhone Battery Health — Is Your Battery at 85% or Below?

Go to Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging and check Maximum Capacity. If it reads 85% or below, your battery needs to be replaced. A weak battery is a risk — it will get worse over time. Signs your battery needs replacing:

Battery drains fast Fluctuating battery % Screen goes dark randomly Phone powers off unexpectedly Shows 100% then jumps to 17% & shuts off Swollen / inflated battery

📍 Come in and we'll replace your battery while you wait.

Payment Policy — Full Payment Required Before Any Repair Begins

We do not repair or fix any device without full payment first. The full repair price must be paid before we start any work. We accept Cash, Debit, and Credit Card. Please note: all card transactions are subject to a 3% processing fee. To avoid the fee, pay with cash.

🍎 Important Notice for Apple Customers

Apple will display a "Non-Genuine Part" notification on your iPhone any time a Screen, Camera, or Battery is replaced with a non-Apple part. This does not mean the part is faulty or has a problem. It simply means the part was replaced with a new component that works just as efficiently as the original.

Receiving this notification does NOT affect the performance of your iPhone. The message will appear on your lock screen for 4 days and then in Settings for 15 more days.
