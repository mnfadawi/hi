iPhone Battery Health — Is Your Battery at 85% or Below?

Go to Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging and check Maximum Capacity. If it reads 85% or below, your battery needs to be replaced. A weak battery is a risk — it will get worse over time. Signs your battery needs replacing:

Battery drains fast Fluctuating battery % Screen goes dark randomly Phone powers off unexpectedly Shows 100% then jumps to 17% & shuts off Swollen / inflated battery

📍 Come in and we'll replace your battery while you wait.

Diagnostic Service

Not Sure What's Wrong? We'll Find Out.

When a device comes in and the problem is unknown — black screen, won't turn on, won't charge, acting up after water or liquid exposure — we run a full diagnostic to find the root cause before any repair begins.

We tell you exactly what the problem is and what it costs to fix. You're welcome to go ahead with the repair — or you're welcome to leave. No pressure.

1

Bring Your Device In

Walk in with your device. We assess it and explain the diagnostic process upfront.

2

Pay the Diagnostic Fee

The fee is paid before we begin. We then get to work finding the exact problem.

3

We Contact You With Results

Once we identify the issue, we call you with the full repair quote. You decide whether to proceed.
