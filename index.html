Diagnostic Service

Not Sure What's Wrong? We'll Find Out.

When a device comes in and the problem is unknown — for example a phone that has a black screen, won't turn on, and won't charge — we run a full diagnostic to identify exactly what's wrong before any repair work begins.

1

Drop Off Your Device

Bring your device in. We'll assess it and explain the diagnostic process.

2

Pay the Diagnostic Fee

The diagnostic fee is paid upfront. We then get to work finding the root cause.

3

We Contact You With Results

Once we identify the problem, we call you with a full repair quote. You decide whether to proceed.

The diagnostic fee goes toward understanding the problem only. Any repair work is quoted separately and you choose whether to move forward.
