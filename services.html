Does Your Screen Need to Be Replaced?

A damaged screen shows up in many ways. If you're seeing any of these, your screen likely needs to be replaced:

Cracked or shattered screen Vertical lines Horizontal lines Full black screen Full white screen Full red screen Full green screen Screen glitching / flickering Typing on its own (ghost touch) Black ink spreading

📍 Walk in and we'll replace your screen while you wait.

🔋

Does Your iPhone Battery Need Replacing?

If your battery is draining fast or not lasting after you charge it, it may be time for a replacement. Signs your battery needs replacing:

Battery drains fast Fluctuating battery % Screen goes dark randomly Phone powers off unexpectedly Shows 100% then jumps to 17% & shuts off Swollen / inflated battery

📍 Come in and we'll replace your battery while you wait.

Not Sure What's Wrong? We'll Find Out.

When a device comes in and the problem is unknown — black screen, won't turn on, won't charge, acting up after liquid exposure — we run a full diagnostic to find the root cause before any repair begins.

We tell you exactly what the problem is and what it costs to fix. You're welcome to go ahead with the repair — or you're welcome to leave. No pressure.
