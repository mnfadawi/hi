Policies & Important Information

Please read before your repair. We want every customer to know exactly what to expect.

🛡️ Warranty Policy

  • ✓90-day warranty on all parts provided and replaced by us
  • ✓30-day warranty on special / custom order parts replaced by us
  • ✓Keep your receipt — required to claim your warranty
  • ✕Warranty voided by physical damage: cracked glass, broken frame, damaged LCD/OLED
  • ✕Warranty voided by any liquid inside the device
  • ✕No warranty on regular wear and tear
  • !Repairing your device may void any existing manufacturer warranty — Phone ElectriK is not liable for this

⚠️ Physically Damaged Screens — No Warranty

The following screen conditions resulting from physical damage carry no warranty:

  • ✕Cracked screen
  • ✕Vertical lines on screen
  • ✕Horizontal lines on screen
  • ✕Black screen from impact
  • ✕White screen from impact
  • ✕Bleeding screen
  • ✕Frozen screen from physical damage

📦 Unclaimed Devices

  • !Devices left unclaimed after 30 days may no longer be held for you
  • !Please pick up your device promptly once it's ready

🔒 Privacy & Data Policy

  • ✓We respect your privacy — we do not ask for your password unless required for the repair and only with your authorization
  • ✓We never share, sell, or distribute your personal information
  • !We recommend backing up your device before service — we are not responsible for data loss during repair
  • ✓Repair details are kept only for warranty and service history purposes

📋 Customer Responsibility

  • !We are not responsible for pre-existing damage not noted at drop-off
  • !Customers are responsible for testing their device after the repair before leaving the store
  • !Any impact to the device can have an immediate or delayed effect on other components
