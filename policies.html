Policies & Important Information

Please read before your repair. We want every customer to know exactly what to expect.

🛡️ Warranty Policy

  • ✓90-day warranty on all parts provided and replaced by us
  • ✓30-day warranty on special / custom order parts replaced by us
  • ✓Keep your receipt — required to claim your warranty
  • ✕Warranty voided by physical damage: cracked glass, broken frame, damaged LCD/OLED
  • ✕Warranty voided by any liquid inside the device
  • ✕No warranty on regular wear and tear
  • !Repairing your device may void any existing manufacturer warranty — Phone ElectriK is not liable for this

⚠️ Physically Damaged Screens — No Warranty

The following screen conditions resulting from physical damage carry no warranty:

  • ✕Cracked screen
  • ✕Vertical lines on screen
  • ✕Horizontal lines on screen
  • ✕Black screen from impact
  • ✕White screen from impact
  • ✕Bleeding screen
  • ✕Frozen screen from physical damage
